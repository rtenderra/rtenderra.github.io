I am a Doctoral Student in Cognitive Neuroscience at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences with [Christian Doeller](https://doellerlab.com/) at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics with [Stephanie Theves](https://www.aesthetics.mpg.de/institut/mitarbeiterinnen/stephanie-theves.html).

My research asks how the brain organizes knowledge into cognitive maps, and if people differ in how well they do it. I combine fMRI and analyses of neural representational geometry with cognitive testing in large samples. With these methods, I study how relational knowledge is learned and generalised in the hippocampal formation and across cortex.

**Background:** I completed my B.Sc. in Psychology at Goethe University Frankfurt, and my M.Sc. in Neural- and Behavioural Sciences at the University of Tübingen.
